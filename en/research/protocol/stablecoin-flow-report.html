3. Three moves worth flagging this period

USDC on Hyperliquid L1 posted the fastest weekly growth on this list (+10.69%). Hyperliquid is an application chain built specifically for perpetuals trading, so fast USDC growth there tends to track margin capital flowing in rather than routine transfers — that correlates more with "more traders opening perp positions on this chain" than with generic demand. Worth tracking in the next few updates to see whether this pace holds or was a one-week outlier.

Solana's USDC (+7.66%) and USDT (-3.79%) moved in opposite directions. When two stablecoins on the same chain diverge like this, the more likely explanation is on-chain swapping from USDT into USDC, rather than net capital flowing in or out of Solana as a whole. Confirming this needs next period's data cross-checked against Solana's total value locked — we're logging the observation here without concluding yet.

USDT on Plasma grew 16.98% in a single week — the largest percentage move on this list. Plasma is a newer chain with a still-small base ($0.76B), and small bases naturally produce large percentage swings that aren't directly comparable to single-digit moves on Tron or Ethereum's much larger balances. But sustained double-digit growth across several updates would be a real signal that the chain is genuinely attracting capital — worth keeping as a comparison baseline for future reports.
